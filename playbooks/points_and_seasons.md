# Points, Miles, and Shoulder Seasons

**What this framework claims:** Two reference tools, not an essay — check before booking, don't read end to end. Points and miles are treated here as an access tool for reaching a specific peak experience (per Bill Perkins' Die With Zero argument for spending down rather than hoarding), not a balance to accumulate. Shoulder-season windows are the calendar-level lever for getting most of a season's payoff — weather, conditions, availability — at a fraction of the peak-season cost and crowd.

> Figures last verified: 2026-11-07. Award programs change without notice — check each figure before transferring points.

## Points and miles — access, not savings

A points balance is a depreciating asset: award charts get worse and program value erodes over time, largely without notice. Treat points as currency to convert into a specific trip soon, not a store of value to hold indefinitely.

### ANA via Virgin Atlantic — the sweet spot for Japan business class

ANA's "The Room" business-class product rivals international first class on some routes — enclosed suites, not just a lie-flat seat.

Still valid, but devalued and scarce. ANA business rose from 45,000/47,500 to 52,500/60,000 on 2024-05-23, without advance notice ([Upgraded Points](https://upgradedpoints.com/news/6-airline-award-sweet-spots-in-2026/), [Roame](https://roame.travel/guides/ana-award-redemptions), [Head for Points](https://www.headforpoints.com/2025/09/22/how-to-redeem-virgin-points-on-ana-and-other-partner-airlines/), [AwardFares](https://awardfares.com/blog/flying-club-guide/)). Virgin's 2024 dynamic pricing covers its own flights only, not partner charts ([OMAAT](https://onemileatatime.com/news/virgin-atlantic-flying-club-dynamic-award-pricing/)). Prices below are medium confidence (secondary sources).

| Central/Eastern US → Tokyo (business, one-way) | 60,000 | 100,000+ |

Booking process:

1. Search **United.com** or **aircanada.com** for Star Alliance Saver availability — both show ANA award space ([OMAAT](https://onemileatatime.com/guides/redeem-virgin-atlantic-points-ana/)). United sometimes shows phantom space, so confirm on aircanada.com before transferring ([Frequent Miler](https://frequentmiler.com/avoiding-phantom-award-space-basics/), [Upgraded Points](https://upgradedpoints.com/travel/airlines/searching-flight-award-space/)).
2. Call Virgin Atlantic to book the award — ANA isn't bookable on Virgin's website. The agent may need to request the space from ANA and call you back in a few hours ([OMAAT](https://onemileatatime.com/guides/redeem-virgin-atlantic-points-ana/)). Transfer points only after the agent confirms the space.
3. Ask about a hold, but don't count on one. Sources disagree: OMAAT's holds guide says up to one day, by phone only ([OMAAT](https://onemileatatime.com/guides/airline-award-ticket-holds/)); its ANA guide says up to 48 hours ([OMAAT](https://onemileatatime.com/guides/redeem-virgin-atlantic-points-ana/)); readers and Roame report ANA holds are unreliable. Low confidence.
4. Mind the booking window. Virgin opens awards at 331 days ([Head for Points](https://www.headforpoints.com/2025/09/22/how-to-redeem-virgin-points-on-ana-and-other-partner-airlines/)); ANA's own members get 355 days ([AwardLocker](https://awardlocker.com/blog/when-do-airlines-release-award-seats-find-premium-seats), [OMAAT](https://onemileatatime.com/guides/when-do-airlines-open-award-seats/)), so Virgin-bookable space often appears closer in. US–Japan space is reported unbookable through Virgin within 14 days of departure (medium confidence, [OMAAT](https://onemileatatime.com/guides/redeem-virgin-atlantic-points-ana/)).

## Shoulder-season windows by region

Target the roughly two-week windows that deliver most of peak-season weather and conditions with a meaningful drop in crowds.

### Mediterranean — September 15 to October 15

School holidays have ended, the sea is still swimmable, wine harvest is underway in Tuscany, and truffle season is beginning.

| Sub-destination | Notes |
|---|---|
| Greek Islands | Naxos/Paros beat Santorini for a calmer trip; 24-28°C is typical |
| Amalfi Coast / Tuscany | Dining becomes accessible without booking months ahead |
| Athens | Rain probability roughly doubles from early to late October — front-load the window |

### Southeast Asia — late October to November

| Region | Verdict | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| North (Hanoi / Halong / Sapa) | Excellent | Cool, dry, clear skies — best trekking conditions of the year |
| South (Ho Chi Minh City / Mekong) | Good | Entering dry season, good for river travel |
| Central (Hoi An / Danang) | Avoid | Peak typhoon and flood months |

Also avoid late January/early February in Vietnam specifically — Tet effectively shuts the country down.

### Patagonia — March 1-20 (the "secret season")

Peak summer (December/January) brings winds up to 100 km/h that can ruin hiking days. By early-to-mid March, winds subside, autumn color emerges, and crowds drop by 40-50%. Temperatures are cooler (12-14°C) but the calm conditions make it more pleasant on the ground than a windy 20°C summer day.
