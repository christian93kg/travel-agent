# research/ — dated research reports

Deep-research reports on the logistics layer of a trip: booking, disruptions, hotels, award travel and airport/rail tactics. Each one keeps the prompt that produced it, the findings tables with their sources and confidence notes, and the source conflicts it found.

> These are **snapshots compiled on 2026-11-06**. Airline rules, fees, award charts and passenger-rights law change often. Use them to know what to check, then check the linked source before you book or transfer points.

| File | What it covers |
|---|---|
| [flight_booking.md](flight_booking.md) | When to book, search tricks, stopovers, self-transfer, fare families, hidden-city risk, seats, upgrades, connections and lounges, Global Entry / PreCheck / CLEAR / EES / ETIAS |
| [flight_disruption.md](flight_disruption.md) | Rebooking during disruptions, EU261 and its 2026 reform, UK261, US DOT refunds, Montreal Convention bag limits, card trip-delay cover, a checklist for a disruption in progress |
| [hotel_booking.md](hotel_booking.md) | Booking channels (direct, OTAs, advisor programs), rate types, elite benefits, room selection, points vs. cash, status matches, apartments vs. hotels |
| [award_travel.md](award_travel.md) | How award booking works, search tools, partner space, surcharges, holds and fees, transfer ratios, and a status check on the sweet spots in [points_and_seasons](../playbooks/points_and_seasons.md) |
| [travel_tactics.md](travel_tactics.md) | Plane seats, check-in and boarding, carry-on sizes, airport and on-board tactics, European rail seats and booking windows, points-transfer timing, a myth check |

These files are reference, like `frameworks/` and `playbooks/`: `plan-trip` reads them, it doesn't edit them. To refresh one, rerun its prompt in a deep-research session and replace the file with the new dated report.
